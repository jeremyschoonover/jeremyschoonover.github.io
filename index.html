Projects

MarchingHQ

Developer & Architect · March 2026–Present

A score tracking app for marching band competitions — like ESPN, but for the marching arts. The idea is score alerts based on your account preferences, plus analytics, projections, a leaderboard, and forums. It's an offline-first Progressive Web App built with Next.js, React, and Supabase edge functions. It's also unfinished.

The hardest part so far hasn't been the code. It's the legal side of letting people make accounts: privacy, terms of service, the nitty gritty details they don't talk about when you're building apps like this.

BandBusBanter & JermVidz

Digital Content Creator & Podcast Co-Host · July 2025–Present

A podcast about the marching arts that my co-host and I started because we wanted to share our love for it. We cover Drum Corps International and indoor percussion — mostly reacting to shows, plus our thoughts on the DCI season. It was just an idea we came up with and ran with.

I also put out solo video content as JermVidz. I handle the full production lifecycle on both: shooting, editing, and post in Premiere Pro and CapCut.
