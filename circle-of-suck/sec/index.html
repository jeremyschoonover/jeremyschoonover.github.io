How it works

A circle of suck is a loop of results where every team beat the next one and the last team beat the first, so each of them can claim to be better than all the others. This is the longest loop in SEC play this season. Only conference games count, and it updates after every final.

The branch is the longest chain of wins running off the circle, so the teams outside it have a place too.

“Who’s better?” shows the shortest chain of wins from the first team to the second.
