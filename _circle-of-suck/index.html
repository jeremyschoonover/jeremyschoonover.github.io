How it works

A circle of suck is a loop of results where every team beat the next one and the last team beat the first, so every team in it can claim to be better than all the others. This is the longest loop in the {{LEAGUE}} this season. Only {{LEAGUE}} conference games count, and it's recalculated after every final.

The branch is the longest chain of wins running off the circle, so the teams outside it have a place too.

Who's better? finds the shortest chain of wins from one team to another.
